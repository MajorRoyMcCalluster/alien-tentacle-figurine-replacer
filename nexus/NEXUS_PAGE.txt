[size=4][color=#6effeb][b]What it does[/b][/color][/size]
A figurine replacer: swaps the [b]Atlas Figurine[/b] cockpit adornment for a glowing alien tentacle standing on a suction-cup foot. It sits on the figurine's usual glowing pedestal on your dashboard, and the top half wobbles on the figurine's bobble joint as your ship moves.

[list]
[*]Cobalt-to-ice-blue body with bioluminescent spots and stripes
[*]Five glowing rings up the tip and a glowing collar where it bends
[*]Wobbles like the original bobbleheads - same motion, much more to wobble
[*]Renamed in game to [b]Alien Tentacle Figurine[/b], with its own description
[*]Original model and textures, built from scratch for this mod
[/list]

[size=4][color=#6effeb][b]Requirements[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*]No other mods needed
[*]You need the [b]Atlas Figurine[/b] in game. It's the easiest figurine to get: buy it with Quicksilver from the [b]Quicksilver Synthesis Companion[/b] on the Space Anomaly. Some reward starships also come with it installed.
[/list]

[size=4][color=#6effeb][b]Installation[/b][/color][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Extract the [b]AlienTentacleFigurine[/b] folder into [b]No Man's Sky\GAMEDATA\MODS\[/b] (create the MODS folder if you don't have one).
[*]Start the game, install the Atlas Figurine in your starship's inventory, and look at your dashboard.
[/list]

[size=4][color=#6effeb][b]Uninstalling[/b][/color][/size]
Delete the [b]AlienTentacleFigurine[/b] folder. The mod never writes to your save, so it's safe to remove at any time.

[size=4][color=#6effeb][b]Compatibility[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Visual only[/b] - nothing in your save changes.
[*][b]Multiplayer safe[/b] - other players see the normal Atlas Figurine.
[*][b]Conflicts[/b] only with other mods that replace the Atlas Figurine model. Mods for other figurines work alongside it.
[*]The new name is in English and US English. Other languages keep the original name.
[*]A game update that changes the Atlas Figurine may need a mod update.
[/list]

[size=4][color=#6effeb][b]FAQ[/b][/color][/size]
[b]I installed it but still see the old Atlas figurine.[/b]
Check the folder path is GAMEDATA\MODS\AlienTentacleFigurine\MODELS... (not MODS\AlienTentacleFigurine\AlienTentacleFigurine\...), then restart the game.

[b]The name didn't change.[/b]
Restart the game. Text changes only load at startup. If your game language isn't English, the original name stays.

[b]Can I get it in other colours?[/b]
Variants are on the way.
